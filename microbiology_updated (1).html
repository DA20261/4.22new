About Microbiology & Infection Control Research

Infection control is one of the most protocol-driven areas of dental practice — and one where the consequences of poor product performance are highest. Our microbiology research provides independent efficacy data dental professionals can actually rely on.

🦠
What We Test

Our infection control research covers the full spectrum of products and protocols used in dental settings — from surface disinfectants to hand hygiene to sterilization pouches.

→Surface disinfectant efficacy against relevant dental pathogens
→Antimicrobial residual activity and contact time requirements
→Compatibility with dental materials (rubber dams, impressions, surfaces)
→Hand hygiene product efficacy and skin compatibility
→Sterilization pouch integrity and seal strength
→Cavity disinfectant antimicrobial activity under clinical conditions
→Waterline treatment product efficacy
→PPE and barrier protection assessment
Why It Matters

Infection control product claims are heavily regulated — but independent verification of those claims in dental-specific conditions is rare. DENTAL ADVISOR fills that gap.

Our testing protocols are developed to reflect real dental practice conditions — not just the ideal lab scenarios manufacturers use for their own claims.
