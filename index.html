Design Notes for Review
🎨Brand: Navy #203552 · Orange #E4762E · Gold #F8C569 · Fonts: Playfair Display + DM Sans
📱All pages are mobile responsive — resize your browser or open on a phone to see the mobile layout
🔍Search bar becomes sticky as you scroll on every page — locks below the nav
🧪Research has two layout options — Option A combines everything on one page, Option B splits into two dedicated sub-pages
🔒Subscriber gate demonstrated on the Product Page — full evaluation text blurred behind a subscribe prompt
📅Award history accordion on the Product Page shows Option C — badge + expandable year-by-year history with publication links
💰Sponsored ads on Homepage and Evaluations are clearly flagged as "Sponsored" to maintain editorial independence
🖼️Product images are placeholders — real photography would be supplied by manufacturers
⚡Static HTML mockups — search, filters, and tabs would be powered by Webflow CMS or WordPress + ACF in the live build
